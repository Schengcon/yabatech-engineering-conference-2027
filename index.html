About the Conference

The 5th International Conference of the School of Engineering, Yaba College of Technology, provides a forum for presenting research, discussing emerging technologies and strengthening collaboration between academia, industry and government. The conference welcomes original contributions that address engineering challenges and opportunities relevant to Nigeria and the wider international community.

Call for Papers (CFP)

Researchers, academics, practitioners, postgraduate researchers and industry professionals are invited to submit original papers aligned with the conference theme.

Topics and Tracks

The conference invites original research, case studies, technical innovations, policy papers and industry presentations addressing practical engineering solutions for Nigeria. Contributions should demonstrate benefits in productivity, local content, infrastructure reliability, digital transformation, energy security, environmental sustainability, climate adaptation, employability or skills development.

A. Transportation, Railway and Maritime Engineering

  1. Railway Engineering and Modern Mass Transit: Digitalisation, High-Speed Rail, Local Content Development and Sustainable Mobility in Nigeria.
  2. The Blue Economy and Marine Engineering: Innovation in Shipbuilding, Port Infrastructure, Offshore Engineering and Sustainable Ocean Resources.
  3. Smart Transportation and Intelligent Infrastructure: Integrating Artificial Intelligence, Automation and Digital Technologies for Efficient Mobility.

B. Energy, Power and Petrochemical Engineering

  1. Energy Transition and Sustainable Power Generation: Renewable Energy, Smart Grids, Energy Storage and Reliable Electricity Supply for Nigeria.
  2. Oil, Gas and Petrochemical Engineering: Innovation, Digital Transformation, Local Refining Capacity and Value-Added Industrial Development.
  3. Emerging Energy Technologies: Hydrogen, Bioenergy, Waste-to-Energy and Carbon Management for a Sustainable Nigerian Economy.
  4. Energy Efficiency and Industrial Decarbonisation: Engineering Solutions for Cleaner Production and Reduced Environmental Impact.

C. Construction, Infrastructure and Environmental Engineering

  1. Sustainable Construction and Building Technologies: Digital Construction, Green Buildings, Advanced Materials and Climate-Resilient Structures.
  2. Infrastructure Development and National Competitiveness: Engineering Innovation in Roads, Bridges, Railways, Airports, Water Supply and Drainage Systems.
  3. Smart Cities and Digital Infrastructure: Applying Artificial Intelligence, Internet of Things and Building Information Modelling to Urban Development.
  4. Flood Control, Coastal Protection and Environmental Resilience: Engineering Strategies for Climate Adaptation, Erosion Control and Sustainable Water Management.

D. Manufacturing, Industrialisation and Materials Engineering

  1. Advanced Manufacturing and Industry 4.0: Robotics, Artificial Intelligence, Automation and Digital Manufacturing for Nigerian Industries.
  2. Local Content, Technology Transfer and Indigenous Engineering Capacity: Building Nigeria’s Industrial Self-Reliance through Local Design, Fabrication and Production.
  3. Materials Science and Engineering Innovation: Advanced Materials, Composites, Recycling and Sustainable Materials for Industrial Applications.
  4. Industrial Maintenance, Reliability and Asset Management: Predictive Maintenance, Digital Twins and Smart Technologies for Improved Productivity.
  5. Small and Medium-Scale Manufacturing Enterprises: Engineering Innovation, Technology Adoption and Job Creation for Economic Diversification.

E. Engineering Education, Research and Human Capital Development

  1. Transforming Engineering Education for the Digital Economy: Curriculum Innovation, Artificial Intelligence, Simulation and Competency-Based Technical Training.
  2. Bridging the Gap Between Engineering Education and Industry: University–Polytechnic–Industry Collaboration, Research Commercialisation and Work-Based Learning.
  3. Technical Skills Development and the Future Engineering Workforce: Preparing Nigerian Engineers for Emerging Technologies and Global Competitiveness.
  4. Innovation Hubs, Research and Entrepreneurship: Converting Engineering Research into Marketable Products, Start-ups and Sustainable Enterprises.
  5. Professional Ethics, Engineering Standards and Leadership: Strengthening Safety, Accountability, Quality Assurance and Public Confidence in Engineering Practice.

F. Cross-Cutting and Emerging Engineering Technologies

  1. Artificial Intelligence, Big Data and Digital Transformation in Engineering: Enhancing Design, Operations, Decision-Making and Productivity.
  2. Circular Economy and Sustainable Engineering: Resource Efficiency, Waste Minimisation, Recycling and Industrial Symbiosis.
  3. Engineering for Food Security and Agricultural Mechanisation: Smart Agriculture, Irrigation, Agro-Processing and Rural Infrastructure Development.
  4. Engineering Innovation for National Security and Disaster Risk Reduction: Smart Surveillance Infrastructure, Critical Asset Protection and Resilient Systems.
  5. Financing Engineering Innovation and Infrastructure: Public–Private Partnerships, Indigenous Technology Investment and Sustainable Project Delivery.
  6. Women and Youth in Engineering Innovation: Expanding Inclusion, Entrepreneurship and Leadership in Nigeria’s Technological Development.

Cross-cutting areas also include technical skills, engineering laboratories, applied research, artificial intelligence, entrepreneurship and industry partnerships. Interdisciplinary contributions are welcome.

Author Guidelines

  • Submissions must report original work and must not be under consideration elsewhere in substantially the same form.
  • Papers should be written in clear academic English.
  • Authors are responsible for research ethics, permissions, data integrity and accuracy.
  • Submissions may be screened for plagiarism or substantial overlap.
  • At least one author of each accepted paper is expected to register and present, subject to final conference policy.
  • Publication arrangements will be announced only when confirmed. Acceptance is not a guarantee of indexing.
  • Registration fees and payment instructions will be published after approval.

Submission Guidelines

  • Submit a complete paper in English through the official conference Microsoft CMT site when the link is available.
  • Include title, abstract, keywords, introduction, methodology, results and discussion, conclusion and references as appropriate.
  • Use consistent academic referencing. Figures, tables, equations and units must be legible and numbered.
  • Paper template, page limit, file type, maximum file size and review model can be added later.
  • If double-blind review is adopted, remove author names, affiliations, acknowledgements and identifying metadata from the review manuscript.
  • Do not send manuscripts by email or use alternative submission websites.
